Privacy Policy for Spelling Bee

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers the Spelling Bee app for Android ("Spelling Bee") and iPhone ("Spelling Bee: Spell It Out"), made by Valerian Gigilashvili.

The short version

Spelling Bee does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your chosen voice on your own device so it is remembered next time. This setting never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Speech voices

To read words aloud, the app uses either your device's built-in voice or, for the American, British and Australian voices, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech.

When a Google voice is used, the app sends only the word being spoken (for example "kaleidoscope") to Google so it can return the audio. Nothing that identifies you is included. As with any internet request, Google receives your device's IP address to send the reply. Google handles this under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The built-in device voice works entirely on the device and sends nothing.

Sharing your results

If you tap Share after a game, the app opens your device's standard share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

Spelling Bee is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.
